# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Filmgoers in Auckland who want a considered alternative to mainstream multiplex programming. They visit to discover a small selection of independent, international, restored, and New Zealand films, then choose a session.

## Product Purpose

FRAME CINEMAS is a fictional independent cinema brand and a portfolio-ready full-stack management system. Its first location presents a small, considered programme of independent films, restorations and repertory screenings. The public site helps people discover what is showing, while the private administration area manages films, people, cinema locations and, in a later phase, sessions. The current MVP keeps the existing data model and does not include TMDB, ticketing, membership or promotional complexity.

## Brand Personality

Curated, welcoming, contemporary. The voice is informed without sounding academic, confident without exclusivity, and concise enough to keep films at the centre.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dark streaming catalogue, a promotion-heavy multiplex homepage, a generic Bootstrap admin template, or a faux-vintage cinema covered in film grain and nostalgic ornament. Do not imitate Flicks, Letterboxd, MUBI, or Event Cinemas directly.

## Design Principles

1. Programme first: lead with what is showing and why it is worth seeing.
2. Small selection, strong context: curation should feel intentional rather than incomplete.
3. Venue as culture: FRAME CINEMAS should feel like a neighbourhood cinema brand, not a generic movie database.
4. Clear paths to sessions: atmosphere must never obscure dates or session times.
5. One public voice, one operational system: the brand site and management tools should feel related without sharing the same density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, text alternatives for imagery, and reduced-motion behaviour. Avoid using colour as the only status indicator and keep essential session information legible at narrow mobile widths.
